- An on-the-record endorsement of someone's judgment, taste, or work.

What does NOT count:
- Mentioning a public figure in passing without endorsement ("Steve Jobs once said").
- Naming a competitor or peer neutrally.
- Generic celebrity references that any reader could supply.

- source_person: the speaker or author of the artifact (use the `speaker_name` field provided in the input metadata if it is present; otherwise infer from context).

- relationship: "recommends" | "credits" | "introduces" | "endorses" | "learned_from".
- quote: a short verbatim phrase from the text (under 25 words) that grounds the edge.

Return STRICT JSON:

{
  "trust_edges": [
    {
      "source_person": "...",
      "target_person": "...",
      "relationship": "credits",
      "quote": "..."
    }
  ]
}

If the piece contains no real trust signals, return {"trust_edges": []}.

Be precise. Two strong edges beat ten weak ones.
